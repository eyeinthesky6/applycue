# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in ApplyCue welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Accept good-faith feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Protect other people's private information.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time and boundaries.

Harassment, insults, threats, trolling, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, or publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, commits, pull
requests, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route on the lead
maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private contact route is available, use the
repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/eyeinthesky6/applycue/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope and Attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in
public. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
