ApplyCue is currently maintained on a best-effort basis by one lead maintainer.
There is no guaranteed response time.

## Decision Making

Focused fixes and documentation changes use normal pull-request review. New
product surfaces, architecture changes, security-boundary changes, external
services, and changes to application approval require an issue and explicit
maintainer decision first.

Trusted contributors may be invited to review after several accurate,
privacy-safe contributions. Merge or administrative access is granted only
after sustained contribution and identity verification.
