Adapt the optional wording and role-family sections to the person; keep the evidence and confirmation boundary.

## Evidence and wording boundaries

Record confirmed facts that are easy to lose, careful wording the user approved, and statements that still need confirmation. Do not copy the full base CV here.

## Recurring perception risks

List only plausible recruiter questions that recur across roles, followed by the strongest factual bridge. Do not turn an inference into a fact.

## Public-asset consistency

Note the current LinkedIn, website, GitHub, portfolio, bio, or writing signals that materially support or conflict with the confirmed story. Recommendations remain drafts until separately approved.

## Open questions

Keep unresolved chronology, scope, authorship, measures, or positioning choices here. A role-specific question belongs in that role's report or campaign pack unless its answer becomes reusable candidate evidence.
