This is an adaptive campaign pack for one coveted role. Keep the five required evidence sections below, but change the depth, order of optional sections, examples, and messages to suit the employer, role, and user. Do not copy employer or peer wording.

## Campaign thesis

In two or three sentences, explain why this candidate should earn a conversation for this role and which three promises the application must communicate.

## Employer success picture

Separate what the employer explicitly states, current public company/team context, agent inference about likely first-year outcomes, and unknowns.

## Perception risks and objections

Name the likely recruiter or hiring-manager doubts, the factual bridge for each, and any gap that should remain candid.

## Evidence used

List the approved candidate evidence selected for this campaign and why it matters. Link to the role report, saved JD, and verified CV bundle where useful.

## Narrative choices

Explain the career spine, role-family lens, first-page signals, evidence order, tone, and any deliberate omission. Keep facts stable while allowing a materially different role narrative.

## Application messages

Include only useful outputs: short motivation, long narrative, cover-letter angle, referral note, interview introduction, or portfolio/case-study recommendation. A separate `application-narrative.md` may hold longer form answers.

## Verification needed

List only material facts or claims still needing the user's confirmation. If none remain, say so explicitly.

## Consistency impact

State whether the tailored CV conflicts with the approved candidate positioning or public assets. Put any optional LinkedIn, website, GitHub, portfolio, or bio recommendations in a separate draft plan; never publish them without separate approval.
